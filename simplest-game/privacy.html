Privacy Policy

App: The Simplest Game (the "game"), made by DrTardis, on Android (Google Play).
Last updated: 20 November 2026

The short version

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. To choose and count those ads, Google's ads software inside the game collects some information about your device, including its advertising ID, as described below. We (the developer) receive none of it: there are no accounts, and the game sends nothing to us. Your score and settings stay on your device.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Now and then, after you close a milestone card, it may show a short full-screen ad. There are no ads in your first 10 minutes of play, and at most one every 3 minutes.

When the game requests or shows an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • device identifiers: your device's advertising ID and the app set ID;
  • your IP address, which Google uses to estimate your approximate location (city or region, not GPS);
  • ad interactions: which ads were shown and tapped, and when the app was opened;
  • diagnostic information: crash and performance reports from the SDK.

Google uses this for advertising (choosing and, where you allow it, personalising ads), for analytics (measuring ads) and for fraud prevention and security. Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): before any ad is requested, the game shows Google's consent message, where you can accept or refuse personalised ads. You can change your answer at any time with the privacy button at the top of the game screen.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your device's advertising ID in Android Settings (Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads). Without it, ads are not personalised.
  • US state privacy laws: where Google offers it, the consent message and privacy button also let you opt out of the sale or sharing of your information for ads.

What stays on your device

The game stores your score, upgrades, total play time and sound setting in its own private app storage. We cannot see any of it. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes it.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's own backup, Android may include the game's save in your personal backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is run by Google for you, under Google's privacy policy. We have no access to it.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads. The game itself uses no online features.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID, and Android's ad services permissions): let Google's ads software read the advertising ID described above.
  • Wake lock and foreground service: used by Google's ads software for its own background tasks.

The game does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage.

Children

The game is not made for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and the game is not offered to children on Google Play.

Changes

If a future version changes anything here, this page will be updated before that version is released and the date above will change.
